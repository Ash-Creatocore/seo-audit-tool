Methodology & limitations

  • Indexing status is a site: query heuristic, not official Google Search Console data. For authoritative status, connect Search Console (a planned upgrade).
  • Rank tracking is bounded by the Custom Search API's 100-results-per-query cap and a 100-queries/day free quota — positions beyond the top 30 (default) or top 100 (max) may not be found without a paid quota increase.
  • Search-volume figures require a paid keyword-data source and are intentionally omitted rather than estimated.
  • Rank history (Change / Best Position) is stored locally on this server and starts accumulating from your first check — there is no historical data before that.
